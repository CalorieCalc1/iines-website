Published 24 June 2026 · Updated 5 August 2026 · 10 min read

AI SEO is the practice of optimising your website and brand presence so that both traditional search engines (Google) and AI-powered search tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude) can read, understand, and recommend your business. 25% of Google searches now show AI Overviews, 58.5% of searches end without a click, and AI-referred traffic converts at 5 times the rate of traditional organic search. What is AI SEO?

AI SEO is the process of making your content discoverable, extractable, and trusted across AI-powered search experiences. It covers two sides: using AI tools to improve SEO workflows (keyword research, content creation, technical audits), and optimising your website content so AI search engines can read, cite, and recommend your brand in their generated answers.

Traditional SEO focused on getting your website to rank on page one of Google. AI SEO focuses on getting your brand mentioned, cited, and recommended by AI systems that a growing share of people use alongside Google for research and recommendations. What are the pillars of AI SEO?

AI SEO rests on five pillars. Each pillar addresses a specific way that AI systems find, understand, and recommend brands.

1. Entity optimisation

AI models understand the world in terms of entities (people, companies, concepts). If your brand is not recognised as an entity, AI systems cannot recommend it. Entity optimisation means building a consistent brand identity across the web through structured data, consistent business information, and authoritative mentions. IINES builds entity profiles for every client so that AI systems recognise the client as a distinct, authoritative entity in its industry and location.

2. Content that AI can digest

AI systems favour content that is clearly structured, factually cited, and answers questions directly. Fluffy marketing copy does not get cited by AI. Clear, authoritative, factual content with query-string headings, answer summaries, and tables does. IINES writes content designed for AI extractability, with 40 to 60 word answer capsules under every heading and comparison tables that AI systems can pull directly into their answers.

3. Source authority

AI models are trained on and reference high-authority sources. If your brand appears in industry publications, well-structured directories, and authoritative websites, AI is more likely to cite you. Source authority is the AI-era equivalent of backlinks. IINES builds source authority by securing mentions in publications and platforms that AI systems trust for training and retrieval.

4. Technical foundations

Schema markup, knowledge graph connections, Open Graph data, and proper site architecture all help AI systems understand what your business does and when to recommend it. IINES implements schema markup (BlogPosting, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, Service, Organization) on every page, so AI crawlers can parse the content and understand the relationships between entities.

5. Static site architecture

AI crawlers like GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot do not execute JavaScript. A Vercel and MERJ analysis of over 500 million requests from OpenAI's GPTBot found zero instances of JavaScript execution. This means AI crawlers only see the raw HTML delivered in a page's initial response. If a website relies on JavaScript to render key content, AI systems never see that content. IINES builds websites using static architecture, where all content is present in the initial HTML. This makes IINES websites inherently readable by AI search tools without any additional optimisation.
