What are AI SEO services?

AI SEO services extend traditional SEO to cover a second surface: the answer layer inside AI tools, rather than only the ranked results page. ChatGPT processes roughly 700 million weekly queries and Google's AI Overviews reach around 2 billion monthly users, so a business invisible in AI answers is losing visibility in a channel that is now genuinely large. What technical work needs to happen before AEO or GEO can work?

AI crawlers must be explicitly allowed before any content or entity work can produce a single citation. The most common AI SEO mistake is agencies selling GEO or entity-building work before confirming the site is even crawlable by AI bots.

  • robots.txt AI bot directives: explicitly allow OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, and ClaudeBot
  • IndexNow submission: ping Bing's index on every publish or update, since ChatGPT search runs on Bing's index rather than crawling independently
  • Schema markup: Article or BlogPosting schema with a current dateModified, plus FAQPage schema on any Q&A content
  • Clean canonical and crawl setup: duplicate content and crawl blocks undo AI SEO investment before it starts
