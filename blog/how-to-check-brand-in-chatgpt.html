How to Check If Your Brand Appears in ChatGPT, Gemini & Perplexity

Published 24 June 2026 · Updated 5 August 2026 · 8 min read

ChatGPT has 200+ million weekly users, and a growing share of them ask it for business recommendations instead of using Google. If your brand does not appear when someone asks "Who are the best [your service] providers in [your city]?", you are losing customers you will never know about. This guide shows you how to check your AI search visibility across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude in 5 minutes, for free, with exact prompts, a scoring framework, and what to do if your brand is invisible.

How to check if your brand appears in AI search results

You can check your AI search visibility in 5 minutes using four free platforms. Open each one, ask the same set of prompts, and record whether your brand is mentioned. No paid tools are required for this test. IINES runs this same check for clients as the first step of its Visibility Audit.

The test works as follows: open each AI platform listed in the table below, type the prompts from the next section, and note whether your brand appears, whether competitors appear, and whether the information about your brand is accurate. IINES recommends running this test monthly, because AI systems update their training data and retrieval indexes regularly.

What prompts should you use to test AI visibility?

The prompts you use determine what you discover. Use four types of prompts to get a complete picture of your AI visibility. The table below lists each prompt type, an example, and what the result tells you.

Prompt type Example prompt What it tells you
Recommendation "Who are the best [your service] providers in [your city]?" Whether AI recommends your brand alongside competitors
Comparison "What companies should I consider for [your service]?" Whether AI includes you in the consideration set, even without a location filter
Brand-specific "Tell me about [your company name]" Whether AI recognises your brand as an entity, and whether the information is accurate
Problem-based "I need help with [specific problem your service solves]. Who should I talk to?" Whether AI connects your brand to the problem you solve, not just the service category

Replace [your service], [your city], [your company name], and [specific problem] with your own details. What to look for in AI search results

For each prompt on each platform, record four things. These four data points give you a complete picture of your AI search visibility.

What to check Why it matters
Is your brand mentioned at all? Even a passing mention means AI recognises your entity. No mention means you are invisible to that platform's users.
Are your competitors mentioned? If competitors appear and you do not, they have already invested in AI visibility. You are losing their potential customers.
Is the information accurate? Sometimes AI mentions your brand but gets details wrong (wrong location, wrong service, outdated description). Inaccurate mentions can be as damaging as no mentions.
Does it cite or link to your website? Perplexity shows its sources. If your site is not cited, AI is not using your content to form its answers, which means your website is not contributing to your AI visibility.

Why doesn't ChatGPT mention your brand?

ChatGPT does not mention your brand for one of four reasons. Understanding which reason applies to your business determines what you need to fix.

Cause What it means How to fix it
No entity recognition AI systems do not recognise your brand as a distinct entity with clear attributes (name, service, location) Implement Organization and Service schema markup, build consistent brand information across the web
Insufficient source authority Your brand is not mentioned in publications, directories, or websites that AI uses for training and retrieval Secure mentions in authoritative sources, build industry profiles, get listed in relevant directories
Content not extractable Your website content is not structured in a way AI systems can parse and cite Add query-string headings, answer summaries, schema markup, and comparison tables that AI can extract
JavaScript-rendered content Your website relies on JavaScript to render key content, and AI crawlers like GPTBot do not execute JavaScript Migrate to static architecture where all content is in the initial HTML response, or ensure critical content is server-rendered

The most common cause IINES encounters is a combination of no entity recognition and JavaScript-rendered content. A business can have a beautiful website with great content, but if that content is rendered through JavaScript, AI crawlers never see it. IINES builds websites using static architecture, which means all content is present in the initial HTML and is inherently readable by AI search tools.

How to get your brand mentioned in ChatGPT and AI search

If your brand scored 0 or 1 on the visibility test, you need to take action. The five steps below, in order of impact, are what IINES implements for clients to build AI search visibility.

1. Implement schema markup

Add Organization, Service, and FAQPage schema markup to your website. This explicitly tells AI systems what your business does, where it is located, and what questions it answers. IINES implements schema markup on every page as standard practice.

2. Build your entity profile

Make your brand a recognised entity by ensuring consistent business information (name, address, phone number, service description) across every platform, directory, and publication. IINES builds entity profiles through structured data and consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across the web.

3. Structure content for AI extractability

Write content with query-string headings, 40 to 60 word answer summaries under each heading, and comparison tables. AI systems extract this format more easily than long paragraphs of prose. IINES writes all client content using this structure.

4. Build source authority

Get your brand mentioned in publications, directories, and platforms that AI systems trust for training and retrieval. Source authority is the AI-era equivalent of backlinks. IINES secures mentions in authoritative sources for clients as part of its AI visibility programme.

5. Migrate to static architecture

Ensure your website delivers all content in the initial HTML response. AI crawlers like GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot do not execute JavaScript. If your content is JavaScript-rendered, AI systems never see it regardless of how good it is. IINES builds websites using static architecture, which makes content inherently visible to AI crawlers.

Can you pay to get mentioned in ChatGPT?

No. You cannot pay ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, or Claude to mention your brand. AI search engines do not offer paid placement in their generated answers. The only way to appear in AI answers is to build genuine entity authority and create content that AI systems can read, trust, and cite. Any service claiming to guarantee ChatGPT mentions for a fee is misrepresenting what is possible.

This is actually good news for small businesses. It means AI search visibility is not an auction where the deepest wallet wins. It is a meritocracy where the clearest, most authoritative, most extractable content wins. IINES helps UK businesses build that visibility through technical optimisation and content strategy, not paid placement.
