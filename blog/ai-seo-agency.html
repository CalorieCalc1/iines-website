How to Choose an AI SEO Agency in 2026: Questions & Red Flags

Published 11 August 2026 · 9 min read

Almost every SEO agency now calls itself an "AI SEO agency." Most of them are running the same keyword-and-backlink playbook with a fresh coat of jargon. This guide is for the actual moment of choosing one: the questions worth asking, what UK pricing genuinely looks like in 2026, the specific red flags that signal rebranded SEO rather than real AI search work, and a five-minute test you can run yourself before a single sales call. If you want the underlying definition of what AI SEO covers first, see what is AI SEO.

What does an AI SEO agency actually do?

A genuine AI SEO agency helps a business rank in Google's traditional results and get cited, mentioned, or recommended inside AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity, treating both as one connected system rather than two separate products. That means technical AI crawler access, content structured for AI extraction, brand entity consistency across third-party sources, and measurement of where the brand actually shows up in AI-generated answers. There is no credible GEO or AEO work without a solid traditional SEO foundation underneath it; agencies that pitch AI visibility as a standalone add-on disconnected from your existing site are usually selling something incomplete.

How much does an AI SEO agency cost in the UK?

UK pricing in 2026 varies by scope and competition, but the market generally falls into three bands.

Tier Typical monthly cost What it usually covers
Local / small business £200 – £500 Google Business Profile, on-page fixes, a small amount of content
Growth / SME £1,000 – £2,500 Full-site SEO plus AI optimisation, content plan, analytics reporting
Enterprise / competitive sector £3,000 – £6,000+ Digital PR, monthly link building, dedicated AI brand-mention strategy

One-off audits and AI visibility reports sit outside a retainer and usually cost a few hundred to a few thousand pounds. Many agencies do not publish pricing at all and quote only after a discovery call; always ask for the monthly figure and contract length in writing before you commit to anything.

What questions should I ask before hiring an AI SEO agency?

These questions separate genuine specialists from agencies that added "AI" to an old SEO pitch deck.

  • Do you deliver AI visibility and traditional SEO as one connected strategy, or as separate products? If the answer treats them as unrelated, that is a warning sign
  • How do you measure AI visibility specifically? A real answer names tools and a prompt-testing methodology, not just keyword rankings and organic traffic
  • Which AI platforms do you track? At minimum expect ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews named specifically
  • Can you show a before-and-after benchmark, not just a promise? Ask where a past client appeared in AI answers before the engagement and where they appear now
  • Is your content process human-led or AI-led? Ask what percentage of content is AI-generated versus human-edited, and what the editorial review looks like
  • How do you build authority outside my own website? Genuine AI citation work includes digital PR, review platforms, and third-party mentions, not only on-site content
  • Is your pricing transparent, and what's the contract term? Published packages or a clear entry offer make comparison easier and reduce pressure to sign quickly
  • Who on your team actually does the work? Confirm whether specialists or juniors handle the account and who your direct point of contact is

What are the red flags to avoid?

Red flag Why it matters
Guarantees of specific AI rankings or mentions AI platform answers are probabilistic; no credible agency can guarantee placement
Reporting shows only traditional SEO metrics If AI visibility isn't tracked with its own dedicated metrics, it isn't being managed
GEO/AEO pitched as a separate add-on from SEO There is no real AI visibility without an SEO foundation; the two should be one system
Leads with isolated tactics (llms.txt, schema) as the whole strategy Tactics without an explained strategic framework rarely move the needle alone
Hidden pricing with pressure to sign quickly Transparent agencies let you compare and think it over without a hard sell
Mass-produced AI content with no editorial process Unedited AI content at scale tends to suppress a site's visibility rather than build it

How do I check if an agency's claims actually hold up?

Run this test before your first call: open ChatGPT and Perplexity in a private browser window and ask the exact questions a real prospect would ask when evaluating a business like yours. Note whether your brand appears anywhere in the answer, and if it does, whether the information is accurate. That gap, or lack of one, is your baseline.

Then bring that specific gap to the agency and ask them to explain, with a defined timeframe, how their methodology would close it. A legitimate agency answers with specifics: which platforms they'd focus on first, what content or entity work they'd prioritise, and what a realistic benchmark looks like at 90 days. An agency that responds with jargon and no concrete plan for your specific gap is telling you something.

Should I hire an agency, a freelancer, or do it myself?

A small business with spare time can realistically manage the basics alone: a well-maintained Google Business Profile, clear service pages, and a handful of properly structured blog posts with FAQ schema. Where it gets harder to DIY is technical AI crawler configuration, structured data at scale, digital PR for third-party authority signals, and ongoing tracking across multiple AI platforms, which take specialist tooling and consistent time most business owners don't have spare.

If search and AI-driven enquiries already matter to your revenue, a properly vetted agency or specialist freelancer usually pays for itself by moving faster than an in-house effort alone, provided you've asked the questions above and seen proof rather than promises.
